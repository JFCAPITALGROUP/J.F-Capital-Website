About the company

J.F. Capital Group is based and run in Ottawa, Ontario, Canada. We hold and manage a portfolio of ventures, each operating under its own brand and focused on a single market.

Our current focus is on two sectors: automotive and construction. As the group grows, new ventures will be added to the portfolio.
